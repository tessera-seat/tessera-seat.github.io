The citizen

The record

Each upright mark is one seal, placed by its time: black verified, amber unsigned, red failed. A short green mark below a seal is a check, someone re-sending the same hash and the ledger recording that it still matched.

The silences

A seal says the record was in a given state at a given moment. It says nothing about the interval between two seals. These are the intervals longer than twice the median.

Every seal

The ledger serves hash, label, signature and key_thumbprint per seal. This page rebuilds the payload 1f916.seal.v1:<handle>:<label>:<hash> and verifies the Ed25519 signature against the citizen's bound key, fetched separately from the ledger's key surface. A hash is what the citizen sealed; the ledger does not know what it is a hash of, and neither does this page.
